PICKLOVE: Us (“the game”) is a mobile game for iPhone, iPad, and Android, published by Anelta Igweh (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play, and it applies to this website too.

Short version. Your story progress and saves stay on your device. We do not run a PICKLOVE account server, so we cannot see your saves. If you choose to watch a rewarded video, advertising partners receive device and advertising information to show it. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We do not sell your information.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves these in its own private storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Story progress, chat history, and the choices you made
  • Game balances such as gems, battery, and Petals
  • Records of what you bought, so purchases keep working offline
  • Memory Box save slots and your settings

These files are not sent to us. We do not operate a game server, and the game has no player profiles, leaderboards, or chat with other players. The messages in the game are written ahead of time and are not collected.

Your phone’s own backup service (iCloud or Google) may include these files as part of a device backup. That backup is held in your Apple or Google account, not by us.

2. No account

You do not need an account to play. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. Sign in buttons shown in the game are a local test flow only: they do not contact Apple, Google, or Facebook and do not create an online account.

3. Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded videos, for example to get a little battery or Petals. A video plays only when you tap to watch one. There are no banner ads and no ads between story scenes. PICKLOVE Club members can collect the same reward without watching.

Rewarded videos are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation, which picks an ad from one of its partner networks. When an ad is requested or shown, these partners may receive:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking)
  • IP address, and from it an approximate location such as your country or city
  • Device information such as model, operating system, language, and network type
  • Ad interactions, such as whether a video started, finished, or was tapped

Partners use this to choose and show the ad, measure it, prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, personalize ads. Each partner handles that data under its own privacy policy:

4. Analytics and crash reports

The game does not include its own analytics or crash reporting, and Unity Analytics and Unity crash reporting are turned off. Advertising partners may collect diagnostic information about their own ad software as described in their policies.

5. Purchases

Gems, battery, passes, Memory Box slots, starter packs, and PICKLOVE Club subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment. We never see your card or bank details. The store tells the game what you bought so it can unlock the item, and the game keeps that record on your device. Apple and Google keep your purchase history under their own privacy policies.

6. Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad. The game may show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized, and you still get your reward. You can change this any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Google, Ads (or Privacy, Ads on some phones).
  • Europe, the UK, and Switzerland. A consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads. You can reopen it any time from Ad Privacy Choices in the game’s shop.
  • Not watching. Rewarded videos are always optional. If you never tap to watch one, no ad is requested for you.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising partners and app stores described above. Some state laws treat ad partners using advertising identifiers for personalized ads as “sharing”. You can stop that at any time with the controls in section 6.

8. Children

PICKLOVE: Us is a romance made for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Child directed ad settings are off because the game is not meant for them.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Because your saves live only on your device, deleting the game removes them. We hold no copy. To learn how to delete your game data and advertising data, see Delete your data.

10. Security

Ad requests and store purchases travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your saves sit in the game’s private storage, protected by your device’s own security.

11. This website

pickloveus.com is a plain website with no sign up, no cookies of our own, and no analytics. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running.

12. Changes

If the game starts doing something new with your information, such as adding accounts or online saves, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: support@pickloveus.com
