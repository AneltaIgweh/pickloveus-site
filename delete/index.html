Delete your data

PICKLOVE: Us, published by Anelta Igweh

There is no PICKLOVE account to delete. The game does not create accounts and we do not keep your game data on any server. Everything the game saves is on your device, so you control it directly.

Delete your game data

iPhone or iPad

  1. Touch and hold the PICKLOVE: Us icon.
  2. Tap Remove App, then Delete App.
  3. To also remove it from an iCloud backup, open Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage, Backups, and turn off PICKLOVE: Us.

Android

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then PICKLOVE: Us.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage (or Clear data) to erase saves and keep the app.
  3. Or tap Uninstall to remove the app and its data together.

What is deleted

  • Story progress, chat history, and choices
  • Gems, battery, Petals, and other balances
  • Memory Box save slots and settings
  • The local record of your purchases (you can bring purchases back with Restore Purchases)

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. We keep no copy, so there is nothing left for us to delete.

What is kept, and by whom

  • Purchase history is kept by Apple or Google in your store account under their policies. We cannot delete it.
  • Advertising data is held by the ad partners listed in our Privacy Policy, for the periods their own policies set. It is tied to your advertising ID, not to your name.

Delete or reset your advertising data

  • Android: Settings, Google, Ads, then Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID). This breaks the link to data already collected.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and turn off tracking for PICKLOVE: Us.
  • To ask a partner to delete data it holds, use the links in our Privacy Policy.

Ask us

If you would like help, or want us to confirm we hold nothing about you, email support@pickloveus.com with the subject Delete my data. We will reply within 30 days.
