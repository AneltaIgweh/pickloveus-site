Help with PICKLOVE: Us

Contact us

Email us and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

support@pickloveus.com

To help us fix things faster, please include your phone model, iOS or Android version, the game version (shown on the game’s page in your app store), who you are playing, and the day you are on. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Club screen in the shop and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Gems and battery that were already used cannot be restored, but passes, Memory Box slots, starter packs, and Club come back.

How do I cancel PICKLOVE Club?

On iPhone or iPad, go to your Apple subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play subscriptions. You can also tap Manage Subscription on the Club screen. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: order history, then Request a refund.

I lost my progress. Can you get it back?

Your saves live only on your device, and we do not keep a copy, so we cannot recover them. Deleting the game or clearing its storage removes your progress. A full device backup through iCloud or Google may bring it back when you restore that backup.

Can I move my save to a new phone?

Not directly yet. There is no online account. Purchases can be restored on the new phone with Restore Purchases.

I ran out of battery.

Battery refills on its own over time. You can also watch an optional rewarded video, use a pass, or buy a Full Charge. Day 1 does not use battery.

A rewarded video will not load.

Check your internet connection and try again in a minute. Videos depend on what ads are available in your area, so sometimes none are ready. Daily limits also apply.

How do I change my ad privacy settings?

In Europe, the UK, and Switzerland, tap Ad Privacy Choices in the shop. On iPhone, open Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. On Android, open your device settings, Google, Ads.

Is there an age limit?

PICKLOVE: Us is a romance made for adults. It has flirting, jealousy, drinking games, and one kiss, and no explicit content.
